# Privacy Policy – ADHD Timer

## Overview

Orangery is a focus timer for adults with ADHD. It works without an account, keeps your progress on your device, and collects only what it needs to sync that progress and manage your subscription. This policy explains what that is.

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026. **Developer:** m45studio\
 **Contact:** anna.krepchenko@gmail.com.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

## Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. The app creates an anonymous account in the background the first time you open it.

| Data | What it is | Why we have it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anonymous account ID | A random identifier created by Firebase Authentication, plus the account creation date | To sync your progress between your devices |
| Sign in with Apple (optional) | If you choose to link it, Apple gives us a private identifier and, if you allow it, an email or relay address | To restore your progress on a new device |
| Session history | Start time, planned length, whether and when a session was completed | To run the timer and your garden |
| Garden progress | Current stage, unlocked items, time of your last session, pending return reward | To show your garden and the reward for coming back after a break |
| Subscription status | Whether Orangery Plus is active; purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat | To unlock paid features and restore purchases |
| Notification permission | Only if you allow notifications, which are requested after your first completed session | To remind you about sessions |

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, health records or the names of the apps you use. Orangery does not make medical claims and does not ask you to record any diagnosis.

## How we use information

We use your information only to run Orangery:

- to run your timer sessions and show your garden, including on the Home Screen widget, Lock Screen widget, Live Activity and Dynamic Island;
- to sync your progress between devices and restore it if you link Sign in with Apple;
- to confirm your subscription and restore purchases;
- to send reminders you have turned on;
- to keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or build advertising profiles. Orangery shows no ads.

## Where your data is stored and who processes it

Orangery works offline. Your timer and garden are kept on your device first and synced to the cloud in the background when you are online. We run no servers of our own; we use these providers:

| Provider | Role | Data involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) | Anonymous sign-in and cloud sync | Account ID, session history, garden progress, cached subscription status |
| RevenueCat | Subscription and purchase management | Anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement status |
| Apple | App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, push notifications, widgets and Live Activities | Handled under Apple's own privacy policy |

These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms. Data may be stored in countries other than yours, including the United States.

Widgets and Live Activities read a small piece of timer state (progress and garden stage) from a container shared between the app and its widget on your device. That state does not leave your device through the widget.

## Retention, deletion and your rights

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. To delete it, email anna.krepchenko@gmail.com from the device you use, and we will remove your cloud data within 30 days. Because the account is anonymous, we may ask you for details that let us identify it, such as the date you started using the app. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to:

- access the data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- correct or delete it;
- object to or restrict its processing;
- withdraw consent, such as turning off notifications in iOS Settings or unlinking Sign in with Apple;
- complain to your local data protection authority.

Write to anna.krepchenko@gmail.com to use any of these. We reply within 30 days.

Our legal basis for processing in the EU and UK is performance of our contract with you (running the app and your subscription) and, for notifications, your consent.

## Children, security, changes and contact

**Children.** Orangery is built for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

**Security.** Data is encrypted in transit, and access to our Firebase and RevenueCat projects is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

**Changes.** If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app before the change applies.

**Contact.** m45studio, anna.krepchenko@gmail.com.
